# PAYJAR Product Documentation

This folder contains the initial product and technical direction for PAYJAR.

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Go-to-market research](./GTM-RESEARCH.md) | Market, competitors, positioning, validation and launch strategy |
| [Product requirements (PRD)](./PRD.md) | Users, problems, product scope, requirements and success measures |
| [Technical requirements (TRD)](./TRD.md) | Architecture, data model, APIs, security, delivery and technical decisions |

## Document status

- Version: 0.1
- Date: 2 November 2026
- Status: Discovery draft; validate assumptions with users before treating the roadmap as committed.
- Initial market: Nigeria
- Initial product: Personal spending planner and expense tracker. It does not custody or move customer funds.

The documents intentionally separate a software-based spending guardrail from a bank/card-level payment restriction. PAYJAR's first release can calculate and communicate limits, but cannot prevent a user from spending through another bank or payment app.

These documents are product planning material, not legal, regulatory, investment or financial advice. Obtain qualified Nigerian legal and compliance advice before handling customer funds or connecting to financial account data.
